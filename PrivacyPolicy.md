## 1. Who this policy applies to

The App supports different roles — **visitor, artist,** and **curator**. The
data we collect depends on your role:

- **Visitors** can browse without signing in; we collect less data from you.
- **Artists and curators** sign in and contribute content, so we collect
  account and content data as described below.

---

## 2. Information we collect

### a. Account and identity information
When you sign in using **Sign in with Apple**, we receive and store a unique Apple user
identifier, and — if you choose to share it — your name and email address. We use this to
create and manage your account and to assign your role.

### b. Location information
With your permission, the App accesses your **precise location** to show nearby exhibitions
and environments and to associate scanned environments with their geographic coordinates.
You can disable location access at any time in your device settings.

### c. Camera, augmented reality, and spatial data
The App uses your device camera and **ARKit / LiDAR** to display augmented-reality content
and, for curators, to scan physical spaces. Spatial scan data and related environment
information you create are stored so exhibitions can be placed and viewed accurately.

### d. Photos and user-generated content
If you are an artist or curator, we collect the **images and content you upload** (such as
artworks) and related details you provide, so they can be displayed within exhibitions.

### e. Content moderation and review
Images you upload may be processed by **automated content-moderation tools** to detect
inappropriate content. This automated check runs **on your device**; the image is not sent to a
third party for the purpose of moderation.

In addition, artworks submitted by artists are **reviewed by our team and may be approved or
rejected** before they are published, so that published content meets our content standards.

### f. Information collected automatically by third parties
See Section 5 for data collected by the map provider and cloud services we rely on.

## 3. How we use your information

We process your information to:

- create and manage your account and role;
- display exhibitions, maps, and augmented-reality content;
- enable artists and curators to publish, review, and place artworks;
- show location-relevant content near you;
- moderate uploaded content for safety;
- protect the security and integrity of the App; and
- maintain, secure, and improve the App.

## 4. Legal bases for processing (GDPR)

We rely on the following legal bases:

- **Performance of a contract** — to provide the App's core features once you sign in.
- **Consent** — for access to your location, camera, and photo library (you may withdraw
  consent at any time via device settings).
- **Legitimate interests** — to secure the service, prevent abuse, and protect creators'
  content, balanced against your rights.
- **Legal obligation** — where we must process data to comply with the law.

## 5. Third-party services and data processors

We share data with the following providers strictly to operate the App:

### Apple — Sign in with Apple & CloudKit
We use **Apple iCloud / CloudKit** to store your account, content, and environment data.
Apple processes this data as our service provider. See Apple's Privacy Policy:
https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

### Mapbox — maps and location services
The App uses the **Mapbox** mapping platform to display maps. By default, the Mapbox SDK
sends **de-identified location and usage (telemetry) data** to Mapbox when the App gathers
your location. You can opt out of Mapbox telemetry using the information ("ⓘ") control on the
map. Mapbox also uses **OpenStreetMap** data. See:
- Mapbox Privacy Policy: https://www.mapbox.com/legal/privacy
- OpenStreetMap: https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright
